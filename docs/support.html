Before You Contact Support

  • Make sure your device is updated to a supported iOS version.
  • For iCloud backup issues, confirm that iCloud Drive is enabled on the device.
  • For subscription issues, use the Restore Purchases option inside the app first.
  • When reporting a bug, include device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Using the New Features (v1.2)

  • Document Scanner: open a folder, tap the add (+) button, and choose Scan Document to scan pages straight into an encrypted PDF. This requires a device with a camera, so the option does not appear in the iOS Simulator.
  • Global Search: tap the search icon on the main screen to find files by name and filter by type. For your privacy, search never includes files inside password-protected folders, hidden vaults, or the trash.
  • Photo Editing: when you import a photo, you can rotate and crop it before it is encrypted and saved. Choose Use to keep the original without changes.
