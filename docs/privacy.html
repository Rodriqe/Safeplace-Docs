🔒 Privacy Policy

Safeplace

Last Updated: March 12, 2026

Introduction

Safeplace is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

User-Provided Content

Safeplace allows you to store: photos and videos from your photo library, documents and files from your device, and folder names you create.

Important: All content is stored encrypted on YOUR device only. We do not collect, transmit, or store any data on external servers.

Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect: personal identification information (name, email, phone), device identifiers for tracking, location data, usage analytics, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or any data from third-party services.

How We Use Your Information

All data in Safeplace is used solely for:

  • App Functionality: Storing and organizing your files securely on your device
  • Encryption: Protecting your files with AES-256 encryption
  • Local Storage: Keeping encrypted files in the app's secure container

Your data is NEVER: sent to external servers, shared with third parties, used for advertising, used for tracking, or accessible to us (the developers).

Data Security

Encryption

All files are encrypted using:

  • AES-256-CBC encryption with PKCS#7 padding
  • PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 key derivation (100,000 iterations)
  • iOS Keychain storage with kSecAttrAccessibleWhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly
  • Unique random initialization vectors and salts

Local Storage Only

Files are stored in the app's secure sandbox (Application Support directory). Files are encrypted at rest and only decrypted temporarily for viewing. No cloud synchronization or external storage.

Data Retention

  • Active Files: Stored indefinitely until you delete them
  • Trash: Deleted files retained for 30 days before permanent deletion
  • Backups: You may export encrypted .safeplacebackup files which remain under your control

Your Rights and Control

You have complete control over your data: access, modify, delete files, export encrypted backups, and complete removal by uninstalling the app.

Third-Party Services

Safeplace does not integrate with any third-party services including analytics, advertising networks, cloud storage providers, or social media platforms.

Children's Privacy

Safeplace does not knowingly collect or store any information from children. The app is designed for general use and does not require any personal information.

Permissions

Photo Library Access

Purpose: To import photos and videos into secure encrypted storage. Only accessed when you explicitly choose to add media files.

Photo Library Add Usage

Purpose: To save exported photos back to your device's photo library. Only when you explicitly export files.

Camera Access

Purpose: To take photos or videos directly for encrypted storage. Only when you use the camera feature within the app.
