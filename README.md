## About Safeplace

Safeplace is a secure iOS app that protects your files with AES-256 encryption. All data stays encrypted on your device—no cloud, no tracking.

- 🔐 Password-protected folders
- 📱 iPhone & iPad support
- 🌍 English & Spanish
- 🎨 Dark mode support
- ⚡ Fast and private
